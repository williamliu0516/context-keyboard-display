context-keyboard-display

Your Claude Code sessions, on a mechanical keyboard's 142×428 image panel. A daemon watches every live session, decides which one deserves the screen, and pushes a frame.

working
which session is grinding, how long this turn has taken, its current TODO item, the diff so far
waiting
a session wants permission — which tool, and how long it has been sitting there
sessions
the switchboard, when two or more want you at once
idle
a clock you can read across a room, plus your 5-hour and weekly usage

You will be asked for your panel's IP address; nothing else needs configuring.

curl -fsSL https://xiaweiliu.com/keyboard-display/install.sh | sh

Or skip the prompt:

curl -fsSL https://xiaweiliu.com/keyboard-display/install.sh | PANEL_IP=192.168.1.50 sh

Two hotkeys, and neither needs an accessibility or input-monitoring grant: Ctrl+Opt+Cmd+K cycles the screens, Ctrl+Opt+Cmd+J walks the session list.

macOS. Installs alongside claude-code-keyboard-status, which owns the session hooks this reads.
